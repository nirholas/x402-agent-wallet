x402-agent-wallet

Wallet policy daemon for agents — budgets, per-merchant caps, per-rail caps, approval thresholds, signed policy verdicts. Pay in USDC on Base or Solana; the client picks the rail.

Paid with HTTP 402 USDC on Base or Solana Your client picks the rail

Free endpoints

EndpointWhat it does

GET /ledgerSpend history, per-rail totals and the policy digest
GET /policyThe policy in force and its digest
POST /verifyVerify the HMAC signature on a verdict
GET /healthLiveness and the payment rails currently advertised
GET /.well-known/x402x402 discovery manifest

Call it

curl -i -X POST http://localhost:4032/policy-check \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{}'

An unpaid request returns 402 with an accepts array holding both rails, each carrying outputSchema.input / outputSchema.output — everything an agent needs to pay and then call the route correctly.
